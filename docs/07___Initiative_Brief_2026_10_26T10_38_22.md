## Part III — Initiative Brief

### 1. Executive Summary

The **Hyperlocal Disaster-to-Commerce (D2C) Mesh** is a mobile-first Progressive Web App (PWA) that closes Thailand's critical gap between flood detection and community-level action. Despite ฿billions invested in national flood monitoring infrastructure — TMD radar, HII's 4,000+ telemetry stations, GISTDA satellite mapping — **72.31% of flood incidents between 2021–2024 occurred without prior warning to affected communities** [source: Stage 2 market research, State Audit Office]. The problem is not a lack of data but a failure to deliver actionable, hyperlocal intelligence to the people who need it most.

The D2C Mesh solves this through a three-track pipeline:

1. **เตือนภัยตรงจุด (Track 6)** — AI-powered hyperlocal flood prediction fusing government data into street-level, 3–6 hour advance warnings
2. **เดินทางปลอดภัย (Track 5)** — Vehicle-aware dynamic routing that blocks flooded roads based on vehicle clearance (sedan ≤15 cm, pickup ≤40 cm, boat/waterway)
3. **เสบียงหมุนเวียนด้วยเงินท้องถิ่น (Track 2)** — Geo-targeted E-Vouchers redeemable at local SME Micro-Relief Stations via Thailand's existing PromptPay infrastructure (74M daily transactions) [source: Stage 2 market research]

**Strategic differentiator:** No existing platform in Thailand or globally integrates early warning → safe routing → economic relief in a single pipeline [source: Stage 2 market research]. The D2C Mesh uniquely leverages Thailand's mature PromptPay infrastructure and the Khon La Khrueng (คนละครึ่ง) co-payment architectural template to make this integration commercially viable [source: Stage 2 market research].

**Year-round sustainability:** The platform doubles as a digital welfare distribution layer for Thailand's 7,800 Local Administrative Organizations (อปท.), none of which currently have a standardized digital disbursement platform [source: Stage 2 market research]. This creates continuous engagement between flood seasons.

**Pilot:** Nakhon Pathom & Bangkok perimeter · 12 months · ≥500 households · ≥50 merchant stations
**Budget:** ฿4.9M–฿9.5M (~$140K–$270K) [source: Stage 3 feasibility]
**Verdict:** CONDITIONAL GO at 70% confidence [source: Stage 3 feasibility]
