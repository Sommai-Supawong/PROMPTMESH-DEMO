! The Problem

Thailand invests heavily in flood monitoring — TMD radar, HII's 4,000+ telemetry stations, GISTDA satellites, RID canal data — yet the intelligence never reaches the communities that need it most.

of floods occur WITHOUT prior warning (2021–2024)

60.66%

The problem is not a data deficit — it is a last-mile delivery failure. Existing systems operate in silos: flood data stays in government dashboards, routing apps ignore flood conditions, and relief distribution relies on physical cash that excludes local merchants from the recovery cycle.
