## 5. Gaps & Risks

| Gap / Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Hydrology domain expertise** — AI models need calibration by flood domain expert, not just ML skills | High | Engage hydrology consultant from Kasetsart University or Asian Institute of Technology (AIT) faculty. Budget for 30% allocation. [source: Stage 3 feasibility] |
| **Fintech regulatory navigation** — BOT compliance is specialized; general counsel insufficient | High | Retain fintech-specialized legal advisor with BOT licensing experience. Krungthai partnership route reduces but does not eliminate regulatory exposure. [source: Stage 3 constraint C1] |
| **Community trust & adoption** — Technology deployment in Thai semi-urban communities requires cultural competence | Medium | Field Ops Lead must be Thai-native with existing อปท./อสม. network connections. Community Coordinator should be from or familiar with Nakhon Pathom area. [source: participant knowledge] |
| **GIS specialist scarcity** — Thai market has limited Google Maps API + flood GIS combined expertise | Medium | Consider remote hire or partnership with GISTDA for technical advisory. Cross-train one Full-Stack Developer on basic GIS tasks as backup. [source: assumption] |
| **Single-point-of-failure roles** — GIS Engineer, Fintech Engineer, and DevOps Engineer are single persons | Medium | Document all architecture decisions. Cross-train adjacent roles on critical paths. Maintain runbooks for all cloud infrastructure. [source: assumption] |
| **Thai-language crisis UX expertise** — Designing for high-stress flood scenarios requires specialized UX research | Low–Medium | Conduct user research with Nakhon Pathom residents during Phase 0. Reference Thai Red Cross communication guidelines. [source: assumption] |
